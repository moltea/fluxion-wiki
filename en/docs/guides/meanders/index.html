The Meanders

The Reckoners' maze: the Wayfinder Cord at a Gauge Stone, the thread back, the Recount of the passages, the Descent Gate, the Summing Lock on the Wheel of currents, traps, the loop's guardians and rewards.

The Meanders are where the flux of the world does not run straight but goes round in loops. In its bends the Reckoners built the Counting Ways — a maze of measuring corridors and counting chambers where every current was set against every other. This is where the order split. You come here for things found nowhere else: one more Hubstone, Reckoner Records and the coffers of their storerooms, the dark stone of the Meanders for building, and the story of why the Reckoners parted into three creeds.

The world is not one big maze but many loops. A loop has an end: a maze of 9 × 9 cells, 16 blocks each, on two levels, and you can walk it to the end. All the Gauge Stones of one land of the ordinary world (1280 × 1280 blocks) lead to one loop; the stones of the next land lead to another. The Codex entry is The Meanders; it opens after Reckoner Records and the Quickstream.

The Wayfinder Cord

The way in and out is the Wayfinder Cord, the Reckoners' thread of Ariadne. It is made at the Flux Workbench from three Knotted Cord, a Tally Tablet, an opaline and an antimony regulus.

  • Descent. Hold the cord (right button) on the head of a living Gauge Stone for three seconds: the cord winds round the stone, knots light up along the pillar, the stone's flux goes into its head in a funnel of sparks — and you go down into the loop of this land. Let go of the button, step more than five blocks away or take a hit, and the descent stops with the knot intact. A fallen stone will not do: it has no head and does not remember its loop.
  • Knots. The cord has 12 knots: a descent takes one, an emergency pull two. Mend it with Knotted Cord on an anvil.
  • The Thread screen. While the loop arrives, brass lines draw the maze by themselves on dark parchment, a golden thread with an opaline bead runs through the passages and ties itself in a knot in the Heart of the loop.
  • The thread on the floor. While the cord is in your hand, golden beads run along the floor from you — the shortest present way to the Tether Hall where you came down. The thread shows the way back, not the way on: the way to the Heart you find yourself.
  • The way out is at the Tether: press the brass post in the middle of the Tether Hall and stay close for two seconds — the cord winds back and you come out at the foot of the Gauge Stone you tied it to. It costs no knot.
  • Emergency pull: crouch and hold the cord for six seconds without moving and without being hit — anywhere in the Meanders.
  • No one sleeps or sets a spawn point in the Meanders: the walls go on moving while you dream. There is no sky, time stands still, the passages are dark — bring light.

The loop: two levels

  • The Outer Ways are the upper level. In the middle is the Tether Hall, where you arrive. In one of the quarters is the Keyhall, a round hall with door niches; at the far edge from it is the Descent, a well with a spiral stair down. The wing with the Descent is locked by the Descent Gate: seals and a socket for a Knot Key.
  • The Heart of the loop is the lower level. In the middle is the Summing Hall, 3 × 3 cells; the Summing Lock holds the way into it, and behind the hall lies the Vault of the loop, a cast cell behind seals.
  • Rooms stand in dead ends: counting chambers with tally boards, record rooms with shelves of ledgers, measure storerooms, cisterns, counters' cells, cave-ins, workshops of unfinished automatons, chapels of the Sum; the rooms hold Reckoner coffers and journal pages on lecterns (hints for the locks and guardians).

The Recount

The passages of a loop move. Every two and a half minutes the loop recounts: in some doorways a shoal grows from the groove in the floor — amber and lilac of stilled flux — and in others it melts away. Ten seconds before, the Counting Bell tolls: the doorways about to close shimmer amber, the shoals about to melt shed lilac grit. The signs show at every level of "Mod graphics".

  • The loop never locks you in. In either phase of the Recount there is a way to the Tether Hall from every cell: the passages change, but there is no dead end without a way out. The cord's thread follows the new way from the next second.
  • A shoal will not rise on someone standing in the doorway — it waits.
  • Shoals and cast walls do not break: this is a maze. Go round.
  • Where the guardian of the Heart falls, the Recount stops for good: the loop is "summed". The entry is The Recount.

The Summing Lock

The Meanders' own puzzle is on the Wheel of currents: to pass, you have to know what a current is made of. The entry is The Summing Lock.

  • A Summing Plaque is a brass plaque by the way into the Summing Hall and by the Summing Niches. Its sum burns in it — the rune of a compound current.
  • Beside it on the wall are two to four Tally Dials. An empty hand turns a dial to the next rune of a Spring or a Pole (six round).
  • To reconcile the sum, press the plaque. If the runes of the dials together are the primal currents the sum is made of on the Wheel, the seals crumble. For example: Brew = Shift + Ember = Tide + Fray + Ember, three dials.
  • If the dials add up to another current — "the sum does not reconcile": the plaque strikes with that current (4 damage) and stays dark for three seconds. If they add up to nothing — only a harmless puff of murk.
  • A sum is what it is made of, not what it looks like. Keep the Wheel open: Currents and the Wheel.
  • Summing Niches are small rooms on both levels with a coffer behind the same kind of lock, of two or three dials.

The Descent Gate

The wing of the loop with the Descent into the Heart is locked: the Gate has seals and a key socket. The Knot Key lies in the pit of the Keyhall under the seals of its guardian: the seals crumble when the guardian falls. A key set in stays in the socket. Any Knot Key opens any Gate: a lost key does not lock the loop forever — take another from the Keyhall of another loop.

The guardian of the Keyhall

Spoiler: this describes a guardian and its moves.

The Keywarden is a Reckoner automaton, the warden of the ways: tall and stooped, of darkened brass and dark oak, a bunch of keys on a knotted cord at his belt and, instead of a face, a keyhole with flux burning in it. The creed of the loop rewound him its own way. He sleeps in the Keyhall by an empty key rack. The entry is The Keywarden.

The guardian of the Heart

Spoiler: this describes a guardian and its moves.

The Auditor is a great counting automaton: he sits over a huge open book of the Sum that has grown into his chest, his four brass arms are counting rods with bone beads, and three masks ring the dome of his head: the Order's of wax, the Tollkeepers' of parchment and the Sunken's, swollen and threaded with mire. Whichever mask faces forward, that is the sum he reckons. Whoever comes closer than 12 blocks wakes him. The entry is The Auditor.

Health, damage, armor340, 13, 10
First halfReplanning — grid lines run across the floor and shoal walls rise from it: for 8 seconds a small maze grows in the hall. Watch where the lights run and walk the dark squares — there is always a way to him. Ledger slam — the book hits the floor, a wave of tally marks runs out. Bead count — the beads of his rods fly in a fan and strike with a discharge; knock them down with a blow
Second halfThe mask of the loop's creed turns forward and he fights with its weapon: the Order's Halt Stones (break a stone), the Tollkeepers' Deduction (casts nearby go into the book and come back as a volley — strike with the opposite Spring), the Sunken's Silt (get off the mire). In a Reckoned Loop — all three in turn, but weaker. The Replanning stays
Rewardthe the Audit Seal; a Hubstone with two spokes for everyone who dealt a tenth of the damage; the Vault of the loop opens and the Recount stops

In a Confluence the Audit Seal becomes the Audit Signet: a ring for the finger, and every fifth blow on the same foe sums the count — it strikes twice as hard and takes one good effect off the foe.

Rewards

  • Room coffers — Quickdust, opaline, antimony regulus, Knotted Cord, shards of the Springs; record rooms hold Tally Tablets and Reckoner Records; counters' cells hold candles, bread and the counters' notes.
  • Summing Niches — opaline, fluxsteel, Lattice and Fray shards, drive drawings, the Summary Record.
  • The Vault of the loop behind the seals of the Heart's guardian — fluxsteel, opaline, speculum, shards of every current, a Toll Reliquary, a Summary Record and an armor trim smithing template.
  • The Summary Record gives notes for every chapter and opens the entry The Schism — what happened in the Meanders.
  • Meanders decor is a dark set found nowhere else. You mine it from the furnishings of the rooms (the cast walls and floor of the maze do not break) and cut one from another on a stonecutter: Meander Bricks, Polished Meander Stone, Meander Pillar, Notched Tile, Ink Glass, Loop Lamp, Ledger Shelf.
Tip
Go together from the same land: there is one loop per land and it is shared — an opened Gate, a reconciled lock and fallen guardians stay for everyone and survive leaving and a restart. Each of you has your own tie, your own descent and your own way out.
