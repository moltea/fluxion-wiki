The Conduit and Lenses

The bracer you pour flux through: cast cost and strength, murk; the Lens Grinder and lens nodes — carriers, actions, cuts, facets and price; blanks, carrier conjunctions, the lens wheel and case, bindings, the Fluxsteel and Still Conduit.

The Conduit is the fluxionist's weapon and tool: it strikes with the land's flux, heals, mines, relays blocks and sets traps. Which of these is decided by the lens in its socket, and you cut the lens yourself on the Lens Grinder from nodes. The Codex chapter is The Conduit, its first entry The Conduit.

The Conduit

A leather bracer over fluxsteel ribs with a flux coil and a socket for a lens. The flux of the chunk you stand in runs through your arm and leaves the lens as a deed. To insert a lens, click it onto the Conduit in your inventory (the old one goes to the cursor); to take it out, right-click with an empty cursor. Cast with the right button.

The Lens Grinder

A grindstone + a pinch of quickdust = a Lens Grinder: the wheel's stone clouds with opal, its axle turns to brass, a ring of runes shows along its side. It has no recipe of its own. Put a blank on the wheel by clicking with it, open the window with an empty hand, take the lens off by sneaking with an empty hand. Entry — Lenses.

The Lens Grinder has no data recipes: a lens is assembled in the window from nodes.

  • Carrier — how the action travels (one lens — one carrier; bigger blanks hold two or three as a conjunction).
  • Action — what it does.
  • Cut — how it multiplies and repeats (each one once per lens).

Clicking a node on the rack lays it on the lens, clicking a medallion removes it, the mouse wheel over a medallion sets its level. Every node takes facets of the blank: the first level its own, each further level adds more; the same node a second time costs half its facets again. The sheet on the right shows the lens's name, facets, cast cost, cooldown and force.

The price of a cut

  • Flux: the wheel takes it by itself while it cuts — 1 flux every half second, facets × 2 in all. At the bed floor the wheel stops, sheds gray sparks and waits for flux to seep back.
  • Shards: every node level asks for a shard of its Spring or Pole (the table above), the Palm is free. A missing shard is replaced by a Snarl Thread — one thread per shard; a node's special item (the ember moth wing of the Courier Moth) cannot be replaced by a thread.
  • You can only cut nodes whose entries you have studied; unknown ones lie on the rack under a lock.
  • The price is taken at the start. A lens taken off the wheel returns nothing, recutting the same lens costs again, and the lens itself never dissolves into currents.

The great blank is a late tier: it is grown from a lens blank by a great Confluence after the Restart, with a Hubstone spoke and the still metal of the Stillness. It holds three carriers, four actions and every cut, and never clouds at all.

Carrier conjunctions

Two or three carriers of one lens weave into a new path for the action — a conjunction. A set not on the list cannot be cut. Conjunctions can be cut once you have studied the entry Carrier Conjunctions; a lens blank holds two-carrier conjunctions, a great blank three-carrier ones too.

A conjunction costs flux on top of the lens's price; one fluxionist keeps one to three traps, clouds and watchers each — extra ones melt.

The lens wheel and case

  • The lens wheel — hold the "Lens Wheel" key (G, rebindable in Controls): the lenses of your inventory and cases rise around the crosshair; let go, and the one you point at slots in. A short press slots the next lens in the ring.
  • A Lens Case carries twelve lenses on the belt, and the wheel sees them in it too (Lens Case). The wheel also sees lenses in a Lined Satchel.

Bindings

A Conduit has two binding slots: a cuff at the wrist changes every cast, an insert in the stock gives a native current. They are set on the Flux Workbench: a Conduit with a cuff or an insert below it; the old one returns to the grid. The binding shows on the bracer and moves to a new bracer together with the lens. Entry — Conduit Bindings.

Inserts are the substance of a Spring or a Pole: Ember — a blaze rod, Gale — a breeze rod, Tide — a prismarine shard, Strata — obsidian, Lattice — nether quartz, Fray — a bone. A native cast (its main action is the insert's current or composed of it) takes a quarter less flux from the land; where the insert's Spring flows in the place, it also clouds half as often; where the opposite flows and its own does not, a quarter more. An insert has its own store of 8 flux, which fills where its Spring flows.

Other Conduits

  • The Fluxsteel Conduit draws flux not from its own chunk but from the nine around — more from the full ones, like a Catchment: in a dry spot with a full neighborhood it strikes at full force. It is poured from a Conduit by Confluence; the lens and the inlay move over by themselves (Fluxsteel Conduit).
  • The Still Conduit casts at full force anywhere and never touches the land's flux: every cast pays from the store in its vial (40 flux). It is the only Conduit that works in the Stillness (The Still Conduit).
  • The Prism Rod is a beam that grows stronger with every reflection in glass, opaline, ice and still water; its charge is Prism from a vial or a Charging Basin (Prism Rod).
  • The Flux Locket charges your things on the move — but only by water or where flux is above the bed (Flux Locket).
