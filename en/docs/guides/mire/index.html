Murk and Mire

What stagnant flux threatens: the mire threshold, how mire grows and recedes, its blocks and creatures, Roiled beasts, effects, and everything that clears murk.

Flux drawn roughly does not vanish — it stagnates into murk: in Crucible remelting, in a broken confluence, in a cheap lens. Murk does not seep to the neighbors but settles where it was spilled, and it takes the place of flux: a murky chunk refills only up to "riverbed minus murk". Settled murk above the threshold becomes mire — a living blight that creeps over the ground and breeds creatures. It is hard to stop and very easy to grow.

Entries: Murk and Mire in Basics, then the mire branch of the Stillness chapter: Mire, Roiled Beasts, The Purifier, Murk Flask, Clearwort, Murk Druse.

Murk shows by the Loupe's crosshair and without it: murk flakes in the air are dusty lilac, the murkier the surroundings, the thicker. A murkcap in caves is a sign the flux has stagnated there.

How mire grows

  • Now and then a seed settles in a murky chunk — at the lowest spot: murk runs down and gathers in hollows. Even more rarely a Mire Hummock settles on the mire.
  • Every mire block now and then turns a neighbor — downward and along water sooner than up: mire runs. Only an exposed block turns.
  • Mire eats murk: every silted block eats a little of it. Mire crosses into a neighboring chunk only if that one is murky too.
  • Mire does not touch lumenash, murkcaps, Clearwort, the Purifier or the grates.

Mire Vents grow on silted soil and trunks: they breathe in four beats, and on the out-breath drive murk into the neighboring chunk and raise gnats. A blister ripens in three stages and bursts with silt or a leech — from a blow or an arrow too. Threads creep across faces and tangle the feet. Ooze runs down into hollows and dries; thick ooze in a murky chunk gathers into a Mire Clot.

Receding

Bring the murk below a third of the riverbed, and the mire recedes by itself: Silted Soil to mud, a Mirebound Log to its own log, crust and blisters to ooze, threads to air, and Silted Stone to Pitted Stone. The place's current settles into the pores of Pitted Stone: a pickaxe without Silk Touch crumbles it to gravel and one time in ten knocks out a shard of the place's current (Fortune adds 5 % per level). Ooze dries into Silt. A hummock without murk withers on its own.

Roiled beasts

Where the murk passes a third of the riverbed, a monster born on its own may rise as a Roiled beast: twice as hardy, hitting half again as hard, shedding murk flakes. The murkier the place, the more of them — up to one in eight. The murk stirs a sediment into each — Brisk, Barbed, Shelled, Mighty, Withering, Filmed, Dissonant, Tenacious, Scorching, Starving, Venomous, Draining, Leechy, Mired; the sediment's name shows above its head.

A slain Roiled beast drops a geode by the chunk's murk: a Murky Geode, from two thirds of the riverbed a Deep Murky Geode, from the full riverbed a Black Murky Geode. Use it to crack it open: current shards, opaline, vials, silt; the deep and black ones add quickdust, a Murk Flask, a Still Grain; the black one an emerald and, rarely, a diamond.

The Purifier

Flux Workbench
flux 20
Place currents:TideLattice

Set a Lumenash Grate on top of the Purifier, or a cluster of them where each touches the next by a face, up to sixteen. Every 5 seconds it takes 0.5 murk for the first grate and a fifth less for each next one (three grates — about 1.2 a turn) and pours as much flux back. Its fuel is distillate of any current but Murk: a vessel, an Alembic or a Gyre beside it or below, a siphon to its side or a poured vial; it holds four parts, and one part lasts four turns.

  • Now and then a grate clogs with silt. Throw a Clogged Lumenash Grate into running water — in five seconds it rinses clean.
  • The Loupe on the Purifier tells what it lacks: grates on top, fuel, or that the chunk is already clean.
  • A broken Purifier loses what was poured into it: it goes into its chunk as murk.

The Murk Druse

Crucible
2Murk2Bloomany heat

A druse grows in three stages, about six minutes each, and each stage drinks 3 murk from the chunk; with less murk it waits. Take it with a Pike Pole: what it drank stays locked in, and a ripe one gives two druses — one to plant again, one to dissolve in the Crucible. Broken any other way — by hand, pickaxe, explosion — it bursts, and everything it drank returns to the chunk as murk.

The Stagnant Backwater

There is a place where murk needs no workshop: in the Stagnant Backwater the water has not moved for years and the murk settles on its own — the land holds a little over a third of its riverbed from birth. Mire lives there through no one's fault, the trees stand as dead husks, gnats swarm over the water. A cleansed backwater silts up again within hours, and the mire does not spread past its edge: the neighbors have nothing to feed it. Nobody sets a workshop in a backwater.

The murky skein

A skein with a thread of Murk in it clouds its chunk and makes mire within 8 blocks grow 2.5 times as often, now and then releasing Murky Glints. It brightens if you clean the chunk while it holds no Murk thread — faster with Halo forced into the skein, or with a Purifier in its chunk. Details are in the entry Skeins.
