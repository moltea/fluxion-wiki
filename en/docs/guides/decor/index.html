Decor

The Decor tab: sixteen pieces of living decor and the vial drop, statues, stained glass, the Wheel Rose Window and 3 × 3 pictures, planters, light, stone and wood, boats, the patina of Speculum-Clad Lumenash.

Everything placed for the look lies in its own creative tab — Decor: living decor, light, statues, stone and masonry, wood and boats, glass and stained glass, planters, small things. Decor is cheap — it's placed by the dozen. Functional things that are also pretty (Orbs, totems, altar fittings) lie with their system in the main tab of the mod. All of them — in the Items → Decor reference.

Living decor

Sixteen things that don't just stand but live: they glow from within, move, breathe flux. Made on a Flux Workbench for a little flux; Codex entries — Living Decor: Lamps, Living Decor: Curios, Living Decor: Yard and Garden.

Color — by a vial drop. Most things have a default current; a click with a distillate vial recolors the thing into any of the 42 currents (the vial loses a share). A broken thing drops with its current. On low "Mod graphics" the moving parts and the main light remain.

Statues

A two-story block on a Flowstone plinth that stands facing you; each has its own stone and one glowing mark. Made on the game's crafting table; Codex entry — Statues, Stained Glass and Planters.

  • Fluxionist Statue, Reckoner Statue, Wheel Statue, Glint Statue.
  • Snagling Elder Statue, Tollkeeper Statue, Statue of the Sunken, Statue of the Still.

Stained glass and picture windows

  • Six stained glasses of the primal currents and their panes: lead cames, panes in the current's tones, a seamless pattern — a wall of stained glass reads as one window. Now and then a medallion with the current's rune appears. Eight glass and a shard → 8.
  • Wheel Rose Window — one picture for a 3 × 3 window: the Wheel of Currents in warm leaded glass. Place nine blocks in a square in a wall; an incomplete square is plain rose glass, break a block — the picture falls apart, put it back — it shows again. At night it glows from within but doesn't light the room.
  • Stillness Clock Window and Snagling Catch Window — two more 3 × 3 pictures with the same rules.
  • Only in a wall; pictures have no panes.

Planters

One planter for all single cross-shaped plants: flowers, saplings, mushrooms of the game and the mod. A glowing plant glows in a planter too, an empty hand takes the plant. Flowstone Planter, Willow Planter, Speculum Planter, Opaline Planter.

Light, stone and wood

  • Light: the Opaline Lamp and the Shard Lantern (brightness — by the chunk's flux), the Firefly Lantern and Float Lanterns, the lamps of the biomes.
  • Stone and masonry: Flowstone and Flowstone Bricks, Carved Opaline and opaline bricks, Reckoner stone, Still stone, biome rocks — each with a family (bricks, carved and polished block, slabs, stairs, walls, pillar).
  • Wood: willow (with Carved Willow and Iron-Bound Willow), lumenash, Mirebound Planks and the biome trees — every wood has its set of planks and a boat with a chest boat: game boats with the Drift sign carved on the bow.
  • Patina: Speculum-Clad Lumenash darkens over time in four stages, like copper; honeycomb stops it, an axe scrapes wax or a stage, lightning cleans it.
