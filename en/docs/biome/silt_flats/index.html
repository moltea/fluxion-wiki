Great DamThe weirkeepers dammed a basin of the Lees to hold the murk itself. The eldest of them still sits on the crest under his gate; behind him the ooze stands to the brim, and below lies the dry bed they drained.
Sunken BargeA Reckoner measuring barge that sank with the murk and settled into the silt of the Lees. Its hold still keeps their crates, and its log still counts the knots of the last sounding.
