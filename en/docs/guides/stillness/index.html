The Stillness

The world of the Great Stilling: the Sluice Arch and Still Grains, the rules of a world without flux, the Thaw Lantern, biomes, stillmetal and freshet steel, the Order's people, places, guardians and masters, the thaw patch.

The Stillness is the world of the Great Stilling worked by the Order of the Still Hour. The sun stands at the horizon — the very Still Hour; water is glass, fire is cold light, dust and drops hang in the air, people froze mid-step. You come here for things found nowhere else: Still Grains and stillmetal, Freshet Ore, the Order's gear, the Hubstones of guardians and masters — and the Heart of the Stilling, the key to the finale. The Codex entry is The Stillness.

The worlds come in order: you reach the Stillness after the Lees and the Quickstream. The Stillness entry opens once you have passed through an arch into the Quickstream and read a Gauge Ring with the Loupe — an arch once stood in its middle.

The way in: the Sluice Arch

The way in is the same Sluice Arch that leads to the Quickstream: flux pays the way there, stillness pays it here.

  • Build a frame 4 × 5 of Reckoner Stone (corners as you like) and leave the 2 × 3 opening empty. The frame plan and "Place a ghost" are on the second page of the entry.
  • Keep three Still Grain in your inventory and throw a pinch of Quickdust on the frame: the grains and the dust are spent, and the opening is veiled with stilled air.
  • Step into the veil and stay for four seconds: the world around you freezes, turns white and falls silent — and you are there. On the other side an arch of stillstone bricks waits for you; stand in its veil the same way to return to your own. You can step into a veil a friend has woken, so two can go together.
  • The first arrival is a reading for the Codex and Deep Dissonance 3 (guide: Dissonance).

Where to get grains before the Stillness: an untangled Snarl and the Seine give them up, and they lie in the chests of the Chapels of the Still in the ordinary world (guide: Ruins and dungeons). Grains found earlier wait for the entry: without it the arch to the Stillness stays cold.

The rules of the world

  • There is no flux: flux and riverbed are zero. The Conduit, devices and the Flux Workbench are silent without a store of their own. Only what lies in a Thaw Lantern's circle flows.
  • Nothing flows: seas and rivers are Stilled Water (solid glass, no bucket takes it), fire is Halted Flame (light without heat), there is no lava, the clouds stand, there is no weather.
  • You cannot sleep, set a spawn point or use a respawn anchor. There are no raids or patrols.
  • The Living Minute: every 30–45 minutes, for whoever stands outside a lantern's circle, the Stillness moves for a minute — riverbed 16, still Skeins thaw, Sentinels wake, grains fall from the sky. More in World events.
  • An Iris shader pack is not applied in the Stillness: it would sway leaves in the wind and ripple the water, and here nothing moves.

The Thaw Lantern

The Thaw Lantern is your light in the Stillness: in its circle everything flows again. It is made at the Flux Workbench; the entry is Thaw Lantern.

Flux Workbench
flux 16
Place currents:EmberLattice
  • A current shard burns in its cradle: a primal shard for a minute, an Ember shard for two; the cradle holds up to four minutes. Use the lantern with an empty hand to see how long it will still burn.
  • While it burns, for eight blocks around water pours, fire burns out, hanging dust settles, Grain Drifts fall as grains, and Stilled Figures finish their step: some crumble into rime, leaving a Still Memory, others walk on as Laggards. When the lantern goes out, the circle stills again.
  • A Stillmetal Vein broken in the circle gives not grains but Freshet Ore.
  • Quickwater from the Quickstream, poured out in the Stillness, thaws a circle of 3 blocks for a minute — a short thaw on the go.

Guard the lantern from Rime Moths (Rime Moths and Salamanders). These moths of hoarfrost hang in the air, frozen in flight; the warmth of a lit lantern wakes them from 24 steps away. A moth settles on the lantern and drinks its store: three moths drink a shard's minute in ten seconds. It is fragile — strike it down near the lantern and the warmth goes back. The Salamander of the Halted Blaze is the lantern's friend: in the circle it thaws, lies down beside it and breathes into it (the lantern burns longer), snaps up Rime Moths, then rests for ten minutes.

Stillmetal and freshet steel

  • Still Grains come from Grain Drifts and Stillmetal Veins (a vein wants an iron pickaxe), from the Order's people and from Rime Moths.
  • A stillmetal ingot is a Crucible boiling over lava: a grain, Alloy and Still. Nine ingots make a block, three make three plates. Stillmetal mends itself while it lies idle; from plates the Flux Workbench makes armor that cannot be stopped and weapons that make a foe linger. The entry is Stillmetal.

The Order's people

The Order of the Still Hour are people, not machines: slate wool with rime, wax on their lips, old brass. A band is a question of "whom first". The entries are The Order of the Still Hour, The Clockbound, Sentinels.

Guardians and masters

Spoiler: this describes a guardian and its moves.

Two guardians hold their places, two masters hold the hour itself. Guardians give a trophy and a Hubstone of three or four spokes, masters one of five; a Hubstone goes to everyone who dealt a tenth of the damage. All of them: Guardians.

The Mute Bell is the guardian of the Bell Tower (health 240). It hangs from the beam of the bell hall; come close and it wakes. It swallowed its own peal, and its hall is as quiet as deep water: watch rather than listen, and jump the silent rings on the floor. In the second half it drops from the beam and covers you with its shadow: get out of the shadow, and if you are caught under the bell, strike the tongue from inside — three blows and the bell lets go. Its trophy, the Bell Tongue, weaves into armor as Resonance, which swallows an explosion, a Warden's sonic boom or lightning whole. The entry is The Mute Bell.

The Prior is the guardian of the City of Midstep (260). He kneels before the Still Gate; come up to the gate and the Order steps out of it in waves, and last of all, him. When he raises his hand bell, his people freeze into guarded statues — strike him while he rings and the blessing breaks. In the second half he sets Halt Stones: inside their circles you slow down and the Conduit is silent — break the stones (three blows or three hits). His trophy is the Prior's Seal: it makes the Prior's vestments and blade (Order Gear). The entry is The Prior.

The Great Pendulum is the master of the Clock Tower (720). The Dead Dial at the top wakes it: set in four Hour Marks — dawn, noon, dusk and midnight. No hour passes in the Stillness, so write them with the Loupe at home (Hour Marks). The Pendulum sweeps the hall — take cover behind the columns in time with its swing. Then the chain snaps and the weight rolls on its own; the Clockbound at the winches wind it twice as fast — kill them; in the last third shoot the catch on the wall: while it is whole, the tower rewinds your blows. Its trophy is the Pendulum Bob, the key to the Founder's Hall. The entry is The Great Pendulum.

The Unmoving is the last master, in the Founder's Hall (1000). The Pendulum Bob set in the dead clock above the gate opens the Hall. Bring four Thaw Lanterns and shards of the four Springs. He moves only while you move: freeze, and so does he. While the riverbeds stand, blows barely get through: put a lantern into a Riverbed Sluice, then a shard of its Spring — the riverbed flows and a layer of his shell cracks. Thaw the riverbeds around the Wheel, not right after the opposite one: Tide after Ember or Strata after Gale is a clash, a burst of murk, and the lantern flies out. His Sentinels step on the beat — break their rhythm; in the last quarter long stillness turns you to glass — keep moving and do not stop. His trophy is the Heart of the Stilling, the key to the Restart. The entry is The Unmoving.

The thaw patch

Spoiler: this is about the finale — the Restart and what comes after it.

The Restart Sluice lets the land of the Stillness go forever — before the Restart and after it. Set it anywhere in the Stillness and put in a trophy of an Order guardian — a Bell Tongue or a Prior's Seal: in five seconds the sluice's clock stops, runes of Tempo gather in a ring, and a thaw patch, a scrap of the Thawed, opens 40 blocks around. The entry is Restart Sluice.

Flux Workbench
flux 48
Place currents:Lattice
  • In a thaw patch water flows and fire burns, you can sleep and set your spawn, rain falls, beasts come, the riverbed is 120, above a river's, and Stillmetal Veins give Freshet Ore. The Order does not come there. It is a place for a home and a workshop in the Stillness.
  • Break the sluice before the peak and the trophy falls out whole. The sluice itself can be taken up and set again later: what was let go does not freeze back.
  • Thaw patches do not merge: at least 32 blocks of still land lie between two. The land of the Founder's Hall the sluice does not let go — only the Restart does (guide: The Restart).
