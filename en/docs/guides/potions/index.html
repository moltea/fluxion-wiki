Potion Brewing

Game potions and unheard-of potions from currents: one rule for all, strength, duration, splash and lingering, the murk price; the tower and the Atomizer; guise tinctures; Current Grit and seasoning.

The Crucible brews the game's potions — the same as a brewing stand — but from currents: no brewing stand, no blaze powder and no separate ingredient for every potion. And it brews what a brewing stand has none of: haste, resistance, absorption, luck. Entries — Potion Brewing and Unheard-of Potions; the Crucible and heat — in the guide The Crucible.

One rule for every potion

  • In the distillate — Brew 2 and 2 of the effect current (some potions take two effect currents).
  • A glass bottle last — a potion floats up. One bottle per throw.
  • Gentle heat only: in a strong boil the potion curdles.

  • Where to get Brew: nether wart, fermented spider eye, antimony regulus. Only the regulus gives pure Brew; the wart carries Ember and Root as well, the fermented eye Venom and Sense. An extra current changes the potion — the Crucible brews the most demanding of the fitting recipes. Watch the distillate with the Loupe and drain what is extra.
  • Tempo is in clocks; the Mixing Vat combines it from Drift and Lattice. Blast comes from gunpowder, Weave from enchanted books and bottles o' enchanting.
  • A dissolved potion gives back less than went into it: the Loupe reads Brew 1 and a part of each effect in it. A water bottle carries no Brew.

Brewing Tower and Atomizer

  • The Brewing Tower brews potions by itself. Teach it with the potion itself: strike the tower with the potion you need (long, strong and splash are recipes of their own), and give it glass bottles as the catalyst. It pays the murk of unheard-of potions just like the Crucible. How to build it — Vessels, Siphons and Distilling.
  • The Atomizer gives a potion as a cloud to everyone in front of its nozzle: put a sample potion in its flask (it stays), a siphon from above pours its currents, a redstone signal — a puff for half the potion's currents. The cloud lives ten seconds; rain washes it away three times faster. The Loupe on the Atomizer tells what a puff costs. Entry — The Atomizer.

Guise tinctures

A Spring tincture makes you its current for three minutes. The Crucible brews it on gentle heat: Brew 2, the Spring 4 and Soul 2, a bottle last. Entry — Current Guises; in detail — Current Guises.

  • A guise takes a little flux from the chunk; where flux is below a quarter of the bed, it fades. To drop a guise — the "Shed Guise" key. After it, no new tincture can be drunk for half a minute.

Current Grit and seasoning

Current Grit is a primal shard crushed by weight: put a shard on the ground and drop an anvil, sand, gravel or a dripstone tip on it — three pinches. Entry — Grit and Seasoning.

  • A pinch of grit in the Atomizer's nozzle keeps its cloud twice as long — for one puff; the nozzle holds sixteen pinches.
  • Seasoning: up to three pinches with food on a crafting table. Whoever eats it gets the effects of the seasoning's currents: Ember guards against fire, Tide lets you breathe underwater, Gale makes you fall slowly, Strata gives toughness, Lattice a store of health. Pinches combine on the Wheel, as in the Crucible. To wash seasoning off — a cauldron of water.
  • A drop of distillate seasons too: a vial next to the food on a crafting table gives it one part.
